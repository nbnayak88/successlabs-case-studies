# Theme A — Understand the Enterprise

> **GICS02 · Fictional educational case · v 1.0 · 2026-10-19.** Enterprise figures are stipulated fictional source baselines or targets. Names, incidents, strategic options and learning exercises are illustrative additions; no actual client implementation or achieved outcome is claimed.

## OMF-M01 — Enterprise Identity & Industry Context

1984 founding; Mumbai and Rotterdam headquarters; 31 countries, USD42 B revenue, 46,000 employees and USD58 B market cap. Materials businesses combine process safety, commodity exposure, product quality and environmental obligations.

**Learning deliverable:** Enterprise fact sheet and sector footprint

**Decision challenge:** Which baseline is stipulated, and which external industry assumption still needs evidence?

**Source locator:** Company Profile; Company Overview. [Evidence register](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).

## OMF-M02 — Business Model & Economic Engine

Steel/alloys 30%, cement/aggregates 22%, chemicals 20%, mining/minerals 16%, advanced materials 12%; total 100%. Segment economics depend on grade, energy, yield, logistics and recycled feedstock. Revenue percentages are not profit margins.

**Learning deliverable:** Segment economics and value-driver tree

**Decision challenge:** Would one margin model fairly represent both cement and battery materials?

**Source locator:** Business Portfolio. [Evidence register](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).

## OMF-M03 — Enterprise Ecosystem & Stakeholders

Board, mine/plant operations, customers, regulators, communities, employees, contractors, suppliers, recyclers and R&D. Source lists 54 production sites, 27 mines/quarries, 14 ports, 38 hubs, 8,500 logistics partners and 9 R&D centers; overlap boundaries need clarification.

**Learning deliverable:** Stakeholder ecosystem and decision-rights map

**Decision challenge:** Who can halt a plant pilot, reject a quality certificate or challenge material provenance?

**Source locator:** Strategic Assets; Business Context & Stakeholders. [Evidence register](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).

## OMF-M04 — Current-State Performance & Problem Diagnosis

Fragmented mine-to-customer planning; inconsistent product/supplier/asset data; limited carbon and quality lineage; manual EHS/ESG reporting; 180+ applications and isolated PLC/SCADA/historian/LIMS data. Source baselines: OEE68%, downtime 9%, first-pass yield 89%.

**Learning deliverable:** Issue tree, baseline evidence request and root-cause hypotheses

**Decision challenge:** How will you distinguish a data-latency problem from an actual process-quality defect?

**Source locator:** Current-State Challenges; Success Metrics & KPIs. [Evidence register](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).
