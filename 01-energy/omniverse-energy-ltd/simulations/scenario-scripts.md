# OmniVerse — Scenario Scripts (fictional)

## OVE-SIM-01 — CEO first discovery
- **Counterpart:** OVE-P02 Dr. Asha Menon.
- **Learner objective:** Establish board problem, strategy and measurable outcomes.
- **Opening objection:** “Before you recommend a solution, what would you need to understand about my business and the risk of getting this wrong?”
- **Hidden constraint:** The business baseline and accountable decision owner have not yet been confirmed.
- **Expected artifact:** Evidence-based problem statement, stakeholder needs, requirements and acceptance criteria, risks, options, owner and next-step decision.
- **Follow-up challenge:** “What specific evidence would change your recommendation?”
