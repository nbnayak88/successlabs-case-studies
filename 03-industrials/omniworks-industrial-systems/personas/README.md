## Mandatory persona contract
Every role-play must declare **persona ID, designation, goal, decision authority, KPIs, pain points, information known, information withheld, likely objections, emotional stance, and evaluation criteria**. Do not assume all personas know the same facts.

## Persona cards
See [simulation-ready persona cards](persona-cards.md) for individual motivations, opening lines and discovery probes.

## Reuse
- Yoodli: assign the AI counterpart one persona and the learner one professional role.
- Interviews: use the persona to generate STAR stories, stakeholder-management questions and objections.
- Certifications: frame scenario-based MCQs and oral examinations without implying official exam content.
