# Scenario Scripts — OmniWorks (fictional)

## OW-SIM-01 — CEO discovery
- **Learner:** Lead Transformation Architect; **counterpart:** OW-P01 Dr. Maya Richter.
- **Opening:** “We have invested in digital tools before. What business outcome will your approach actually change?”
- **Hidden context for persona:** Board is skeptical of unverified benefit forecasts and inconsistent customer asset records.
- **Learner must elicit:** priority customer outcomes, baseline, risk appetite, board decision, operating model options.
- **Expected artifact:** one-page discovery brief with problem, stakeholders, assumptions, success measures, next interviews.
- **Follow-up objection:** “Why not let each plant solve its own problems?”

## OW-SIM-02 — Field service requirements
- **Learner:** Business Analyst / Solution Architect; **counterparts:** OW-P12 Daniel Okafor and OW-P05 Priya Nair.
- **Opening:** “Our technicians open five systems to troubleshoot one asset. Tell us what you need to know before recommending software.”
- **Hidden context:** Missing configuration-to-serial mapping and unreliable offline access in remote locations.
- **Elicit functional requirements:** asset lookup, configuration, work order, warranty, parts availability, diagnostic history.
- **Elicit nonfunctional requirements:** offline mode, latency, identity, auditability, data quality, availability and safety.
- **Acceptance criteria example (illustrative):** authorized technician retrieves asset history from a unique asset identifier, with auditable access and defined offline behavior.
- **Expected artifact:** prioritized requirement catalog, user stories, traceability matrix and open questions.
- **Follow-up objection:** “What happens when connectivity fails at a customer site?”

## OW-SIM-03 — CFO business-case challenge
- **Counterpart:** OW-P03 Sofia Laurent.
- **Opening:** “You show USD 575M investment and USD 465M annual benefits. Where is the cash flow model?”
- **Hidden context:** Annual benefit amounts are not yet phased or independently validated.
- **Expected response:** distinguish annual run-rate from cumulative benefit, challenge overlap, quantify downside and request discount rate, OPEX, tax, ramp and attribution assumptions.
- **Expected artifact:** financial assumption register and sensitivity scenarios.

## OW-SIM-04 — IT/OT design authority
- **Counterparts:** OW-P04 Dr. Lukas Weber and OW-P06 Marcus Vogel.
- **Opening:** “Your cloud design wants plant data. Explain exactly how you prevent production disruption.”
- **Expected response:** zone/conduit segmentation, least privilege, industrial protocols, fail-safe edge operation, monitoring, rollback and safety sign-off.
- **Expected artifact:** conceptual trust-boundary diagram and architecture decision record.

## OW-SIM-05 — Customer recovery
- **Counterpart:** OW-P13 Elena Brooks.
- **Opening:** “Three repeat failures and no complete service history. Why should we renew?”
- **Expected response:** acknowledge impact without inventing causes, commit to evidence gathering, containment, accountable communications and measurable corrective action.
- **Expected artifact:** customer recovery action plan and root-cause investigation charter.

## OW-SIM-06 — Executive steering
- **Counterparts:** OW-P02 Arjun Mehta and OW-P11 Hannah Fischer.
- **Opening:** “We have three strategic options and limited delivery capacity. Which option do you recommend?”
- **Expected response:** compare lighthouse, platform-first and federated options against value, risk, speed, standards, adoption and dependencies.
- **Expected artifact:** weighted option decision and milestone-based funding proposal.

## OW-SIM-07 — CHRO adoption
- **Counterpart:** OW-P07 Dr. Anika Shah.
- **Opening:** “Why will frontline teams adopt a new workflow when the old one still works?”
- **Expected artifact:** role-based change impacts, adoption measures, skill plans and sponsor actions.

## OW-SIM-08 — Supplier traceability
- **Counterparts:** OW-P14 Noor Al-Farsi and OW-P08 Chen Wei.
- **Opening:** “Why do you need our batch-level records, and who can access them?”
- **Expected artifact:** data-sharing purpose, minimal data contract, governance and supplier benefit statement.

## OW-SIM-09 — Architecture certification rehearsal
- **Counterpart:** OW-P11 Hannah Fischer.
- **Opening:** “Differentiate a capability, a value stream, a business process and an application using OmniWorks.”
- **Expected response:** accurate definitions, cross-layer traceability and practical examples; cite actual exam-body materials for exam-specific rules.
- **Expected artifact:** structured oral response and improvement log.

## OW-SIM-10 — Consulting interview rehearsal
- **Counterpart:** OW-P02 Arjun Mehta.
- **Opening:** “Tell me about a time you reconciled competing stakeholder requirements.”
- **Expected response:** STAR format using a clearly labeled **hypothetical case**, not a fabricated personal employment claim.
- **Expected artifact:** 90-second STAR answer, evidence and lessons.
