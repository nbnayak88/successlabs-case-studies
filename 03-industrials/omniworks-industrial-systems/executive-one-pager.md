# OmniWorks Industrial Systems Ltd. — Executive One-Page Case Study

**GICS03 · Industrials | Business Transformation | Executive Brief v1.0 | Fictional learning case**

> **Hero story:** A trusted global equipment manufacturer faces repeat customer asset failures, fragmented engineering-to-service information, and growing pressure to sell **uptime rather than equipment**. CEO **Dr. Maya Richter (fictional)** must decide how to turn a USD 575M transformation envelope into credible customer, operational and financial value.

| **Enterprise at a glance** | **Fictional case baseline** |
|---|---|
| Business | Industrial automation (27%), aerospace and defence (23%), transport and mobility (19%), infrastructure (16%), aftermarket and lifecycle services (15%) |
| Scale | USD 48B revenue; 74,000 employees; 36 countries; 61 plants/engineering/assembly facilities; 320 service depots; 7,200 suppliers |
| Strategic ambition | Connected, safe and sustainable industrial ecosystem with outcome-led lifecycle services |
| Board challenge | Improve reliability and service revenue without disrupting safety-critical operations or proliferating disconnected platforms |

## 1. Why change? — The burning platform
- **Customer reliability:** recurring transport asset failures cannot be consistently traced across configuration, telemetry, supplier batches and maintenance history.
- **Operational fragmentation:** engineering, factories, field service and suppliers use disconnected systems and processes.
- **Economic pressure:** hardware-centric revenue must evolve toward higher-value service relationships.
- **Delivery risk:** industrial cybersecurity, plant uptime, workforce adoption and benefit attribution cannot be afterthoughts.

## 2. What is the transformation?
**North Star:** *From product-centric manufacturer to connected, service-led industrial technology enterprise.*

| Strategic pillar | High-level response | Business outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Digital thread | Connect PLM, ERP, MES, EAM, field service and governed Product/Asset/Service 360 | Traceability and faster root-cause analysis |
| Smart industrial operations | OT connectivity, condition monitoring, AI quality inspection and digital twins | Reliability, quality and productivity |
| Outcome-led customer services | Connected assets, technician mobility, predictive service and uptime contracts | First-time fix and recurring service growth |
| Resilient ecosystem | Supplier intelligence, secure partner integration and traceable parts | Fewer disruptions and quality escapes |
| People, risk and governance | Value-stream ownership, cybersecurity, workforce upskilling and benefits assurance | Safe adoption and measurable value |

## 3. How will executives decide?
**Illustrative options:** A. Local lighthouse pilots; B. Enterprise platform first; C. **Federated value-stream transformation** with shared enterprise standards and staged business-led delivery (**provisional recommendation**). Validate with financial, architecture, cyber, plant safety and adoption gates before approval.

**Illustrative 36-month sequence:** Year 1 — data/OT/cyber foundation and pilots; Year 2 — scale digital thread, quality and service mobility; Year 3 — industrial twins, optimization and outcome-based services.

## 4. What is the value?
| Measure | Fictional baseline | Year 3 target — **not achieved** |
|---|---:|---:|
| Overall equipment effectiveness | 70% | 85% |
| Unplanned downtime | 8% | 2.5% |
| First-pass yield | 88% | 97% |
| First-time fix rate | 72% | 90% |
| Service revenue mix | 15% | 28% |
| Energy intensity index | 100 | 75 |

**Illustrative economics:** USD **575M** three-year investment envelope; USD **465M** sum of stated *annual benefit categories* (not a verified net annual cash flow or guaranteed ROI). Finance must reconcile phasing, incremental benefit, operating costs, double counting, NPV and payback.

## 6. Executive decision requested
**Approve a staged discovery and architecture-validation phase**, not an unconditional rollout: confirm the enterprise baselines, choose the operating model, assign capability/value-stream owners, establish plant and cybersecurity guardrails, and authorize a financially measurable pilot.
