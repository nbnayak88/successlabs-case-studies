# Hero Story — The Referral Without an Owner

> **GICS06 · Health Care · Fictional educational case · v1.0 · 2026-10-19.** Enterprise figures are source-stipulated baselines or targets. New names, incidents, options and exercises are illustrative. This case is architecture learning, not clinical guidance or evidence of a deployed clinical system.

## Prologue
In a fictional coordination exercise, a referral crosses facilities without a clear owner. The patient repeats information while an operational dashboard shows conflicting episode status. These invented events are discovery triggers, not clinical facts or a diagnosis.

## Act I — Understand
CEO Ananya Raman asks how a126,000-person healthcare group can coordinate access across19countries without equating digital activity with better care. (OHS-M01–04)

## Act II — Choose
Chief Medical Officer Dr.Leila Morgan protects professional authority; experience lead Elena Brooks asks whose access is missing; CFO Michael Chen demands measurable value. Compare local pilots, platform first and federated journey experiments. (OHS-M05–08)

## Act III — Architect
Identity lead Priya Nair and architect Daniel Weber trace patient, episode, workflow and data ownership. CISO Omar Haddad challenges unnecessary access. A standards label does not prove that a handoff works. (OHS-M09–12)

## Act IV — Fund evidence
Source proposes620M over3years and250Mannual value. Finance requires cost, capacity-to-value and overlap evidence before commitment. Identity/interoperability/access foundations precede safe expansion. (OHS-M13–16)

## Act V — Make change usable
Care coordinator Dr.Amina Okafor and CHRO Nina Fernandes test role learning and workload. Operational lead Dr.Arjun Mehta requires reliable status, escalation and downtime behavior. No clinical actions are automated in this learning pilot. (OHS-M17–19)

## Act VI — Prove or redesign
NPS, waits, readmissions, burden, productivity and cyber resilience need owners, definitions and evidence. Source targets remain aspirations. The board scales, redesigns or stops; no historical success is invented. (OHS-M20–22)
