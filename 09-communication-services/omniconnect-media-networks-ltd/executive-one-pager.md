# OmniConnect — Executive One-Page Brief

> **GICS09 · Communication Services · Fictional educational case · v1.0 · 2026-10-19.** Source figures are fictional enterprise baselines or targets. New names, incidents, options and exercises are illustrative. No approved investment, tested production network, standards conformance or achieved outcome is asserted.

**Decision:** Fund a bounded converged-activation pilot with privacy, service-state, recovery and economic evidence before expansion.

**Situation:** USD37B communications group;62,000employees;28countries;210Msubscriptions. Mobile34%, fixed/fiber25%, media19%, enterprise14%, advertising/data8%.

**Source problem:** Separate network, customer, billing, content/ad stacks; manual activation and assurance; identity/consent silos; traffic-driven cost/energy pressure.

**Illustrative trigger:** A converged bundle shows billing active while connectivity and content entitlement remain incomplete. Care and network teams cannot reconcile status. Invented discovery scenario, not an actual outage.

**Options:** Local domain pilots; central platform/network first; federated service pilots with common identity, catalog, telemetry, privacy and resilience. Third option provisional.

**Architecture:** Discover→Subscribe→Activate→Connect→Engage→Support→Retain. Subscriber/Service/Network/Content/Partner360; BSS/OSS/CRM/billing/CMS/ad-tech; API/event contracts and governed telemetry. TM Forum labels are source proposals, not tested conformance.

**Economics:** ProposedUSD1.1B over3years; USD410Mannual value assumption. Split, phasing, costs, overlap, margin/cash conversion and discounting absent; no validated NPV/payback.

|Source target|Evidence gap|
|---|---|
|Year1:20%priority functions virtualized|Population/effective-operation criteria|
|Year2:25%faster fulfillment;15%fewer incidents|Baseline, cohort, method|
|Year3:40%zero-touch;20%lower energy perTB|Exceptions, permissions, energy boundary|
|Other:NPS>55;99.99%core availability;30%faster activation;20%engagement lift;18%cost reduction|Distinct scopes, definitions and attribution|

**Gates:** Order/service/billing/rights reconciliation, consent, permissions, simulated recovery, role readiness and subscriber value.
