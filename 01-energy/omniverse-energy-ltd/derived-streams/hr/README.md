## Fast general use case — OVE-HR-UC01
Nina Kohli (OVE-P01) must make workforce readiness a dependable enabler of energy transition. A proposed pilot connects a refinery population and renewable-project team. Operations wants qualified crews available; employees want correct pay and transparent careers; Finance wants controlled spending. Fragmented employment, credentials, time and payroll records make speed alone a poor measure of success.

Anika Sharma owns the employment foundation. Arjun Malhotra secures fairly assessed recruits; Priya Menon orchestrates readiness; Dr. Kabir Sen verifies learning pathways; Vikram Deshpande controls time inputs; the HR07 payroll persona Meera Iyer reconciles pay. Maya Krishnan, Dr. Elena Rao and Rhea Narayanan connect sustainable performance, development and rewards. Dr. Ananya Bose supplies governed measures, Neha Varma makes services usable, Dr. Tara Menon governs assistance, and Sameer Kulkarni makes exchanges observable.

**Decision:** compare a rapid uniform rollout, isolated local implementations, and a federated global core with governed local variants. The third is a learning hypothesis, subject to evidence and stage gates. A badge or course completion alone never authorizes restricted work. Proposed result: workers are ready, paid correctly and able to grow; no achieved improvement is claimed.

**Enterprise facts inherited:** 68,000 employees, 42 countries, Mumbai/Houston headquarters. Do not allocate the enterprise USD900M to HR without approval. Benefits remain within HR01/HR05/HR12 interfaces under OVE-P07 Dennis Abbott; they are not silently added as a fourteenth case.
