## Prologue — A service with conflicting states
An invented converged bundle is billed active while connectivity and content entitlement are incomplete. Care, network and billing tools disagree. This prompts discovery, not a claim about a real outage or proven cause.

## Act I — Understand the Enterprise (OCN-M01–04)
Fictional CEO **Anika Rahman (OCN-P01)** leads a source-stipulated **USD37B** group with **62,000employees,28countries and210Mmobile/broadband/media subscriptions**, headquartered in Toronto/Dubai. Subscriptions do not necessarily equal unique people.
Mix:mobile34%, fixed/fiber25%, media19%, enterprise14%, advertising/data8%.
Source problems:fragmented domains, manual orchestration, identity/consent silos andtraffic-driven cost/energy pressure.
[Enterprise theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/09-communication-services/omniconnect-media-networks-ltd/master/theme-a-enterprise.md)

## Act II — Choose the Future (OCN-M05–08)
Convergence chief **Maya Patel (OCN-P03)** asks for simplicity; network chief **Arjun Mehta (OCN-P04)** requires operational authority; CFO **David Chen (OCN-P02)** challenges economics.
Compare domain-local pilots, central platform/network first and federated service experiments with common identity, catalog, privacy and resilience. Third option provisional.
NPS>55,99.99%core availability,30%faster activation,20%higher engagement and18%lower cost remain undefined source ambitions.
[Strategy theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/09-communication-services/omniconnect-media-networks-ltd/master/theme-b-strategy.md)

## Act III — Architect Reconciled Services (OCN-M09–12)
Source chain: **Discover → Subscribe → Activate → Connect → Engage → Support → Retain**.
Fulfillment lead **Priya Shah (OCN-P13)** and architect **Daniel Weber (OCN-P12)** connect Subscriber/Service/Network/Content/Partner360, BSS/OSS/CRM/billing/CMS/ad-tech, API/event contracts and telemetry.
**Mei Lin (OCN-P09)** and **Omar Haddad (OCN-P10)** require consent, rights, permissions and recoverable boundaries. TM Forum/API/cloud-native patterns are source proposals, not conformance or production evidence.
[Architecture theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/09-communication-services/omniconnect-media-networks-ltd/master/theme-c-architecture.md)

## Act IV — Fund and Sequence Evidence (OCN-M13–16)
Source proposes **USD1.1B over3years** and **USD410M annual value**. Components, run cost, overlap, retention/revenue-to-margin, phasing and discounting absent; NPV/payback unvalidated.
Year1 identity, network data, core pilots → Year2 AI assurance, digital activation, converged offers → Year3 network APIs, edge and autonomous-operation proposals.
Targets:20%priority functions virtualized;25%faster fulfillment/15%fewer incidents;40%zero-touch/20%lower energy perTB.
[Transformation theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/09-communication-services/omniconnect-media-networks-ltd/master/theme-d-transformation.md)

## Act V — Make Change Usable and Recoverable (OCN-M17–19)
Assurance director **Marcus Okoro (OCN-P08)** asks whether subscribers actually recovered. CHRO **Nadia Fernandes (OCN-P07)** requires field/care learning and escalation support.
Illustrative first pilot uses synthetic data and simulated network actions. Test partial activation, identity/rights/consent, duplicates/late events, telemetry, reconciliation, stop/rollback and support. No production network action executed.
[Execution theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/09-communication-services/omniconnect-media-networks-ltd/master/theme-e-execution.md)

## Act VI — Prove Subscriber Value (OCN-M20–22)
Availability, activation, digital-care resolution, churn, engagement, energy perTB andAPI revenue need baselines, owners, cohorts and balancing measures.
**30%faster activation versus25%faster fulfillment** remains a scope/time/definition gap. Core availability differs from full experience; energy intensity differs from absolute energy. All future figures are targets, not results.
[Value theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/09-communication-services/omniconnect-media-networks-ltd/master/theme-f-value.md)
