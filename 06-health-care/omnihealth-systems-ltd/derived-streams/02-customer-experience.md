# OmniHealth — Patient / Customer Experience Derived Case

> **GICS06 · Health Care · Fictional educational case · v1.0 · 2026-10-19.** Enterprise figures are source-stipulated baselines or targets. New names, incidents, options and exercises are illustrative. This case is architecture learning, not clinical guidance or evidence of a deployed clinical system.

**Status:** Initial scope blueprint.
- **Sponsors:** OHS-P08, OHS-P16.
- **Master:** OHS-M03, OHS-M06, OHS-M10, OHS-M20.
- **Scope:** Accessible scheduling, referrals, communication, feedback and non-digital alternatives.
- **Value focus:** Access-to-Coordinated-Care.
- **Learning outputs:** Discovery, stakeholder simulation, capability/workflow map, requirements, architecture decision, pilot acceptance and benefits reflection.
- **Experiment:** Observe one non-clinical handoff, test a bounded change with approved test data, and defend scale/redesign/stop.
