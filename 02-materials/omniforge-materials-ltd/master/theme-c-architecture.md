# Theme C — Architect the Future Enterprise

> **GICS02 · Fictional educational case · v 1.0 · 2026-10-19.** Enterprise figures are stipulated fictional source baselines or targets. Names, incidents, strategic options and learning exercises are illustrative additions; no actual client implementation or achieved outcome is claimed.

## OMF-M09 — Business Capability Architecture

Source maturity: asset management L2→L4, production optimization L2→L4, quality L2→L4, supply-chain planning L2→L4, sustainability reporting L1→L4. Scale definitions and evidence must be created; L4 does not authorize autonomous safety-critical control.

**Learning deliverable:** Capability map, maturity rubric and heatmap

**Decision challenge:** Which missing capability blocks material passports even if the portal is ready?

**Source locator:** Core Business Capabilities. [Evidence register](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).

## OMF-M10 — Value Streams & Process Architecture

Source chain Explore→Extract→Process→Manufacture→Distribute→Recover→Reuse. Illustrative cross-enterprise streams: Feedstock-to-Qualified-Material, Asset-to-Reliability, Order-to-Trusted-Delivery, Recover-to-Reuse and Evidence-to-Carbon-Claim.

**Learning deliverable:** Stage-capability/process map and material genealogy

**Decision challenge:** Where do splits, merges, rework and recycled inputs break batch genealogy?

**Source locator:** Value Stream Mapping; Material Events; 14.3. [Evidence register](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).

## OMF-M11 — Target Operating Model & Organization

Source calls for a Chief Digital and Sustainability Officer, cross-functional steering and plant product teams. Central definitions and assurance coexist with local process ownership, plant stop authority and commercial accountability.

**Learning deliverable:** TOM, RACI, plant authority and product ownership

**Decision challenge:** Who owns an incorrect carbon certificate when data comes from three plants?

**Source locator:** Leadership & Governance; Operating Model Evolution. [Evidence register](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).

## OMF-M12 — Enterprise Architecture & Digital Enablement

Source architecture: Asset/Material/Product/Supplier/Customer 360, ERP/MES/EAM/LIMS/mine planning, APIs/events/B2 B and governed data products; edge for plant controls and hybrid cloud for enterprise workloads. Vendor names are source architecture proposals, not validated selections. Carbon and quality evidence require lineage.

**Learning deliverable:** Conceptual architecture, trust boundaries and lineage design

**Decision challenge:** How will the plant remain safe during cloud or network loss?

**Source locator:** Phase C; Phase D; Integration & Interoperability. [Evidence register](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).
