## Prologue — A coordination gap
An invented referral crosses facilities without a clear owner. The patient repeats information; an operational dashboard cannot reconcile episode status. This prompts discovery, not a clinical diagnosis or claim of an actual incident.
## Act I — Understand the Enterprise (OHS-M01–04)
Fictional CEO **Ananya Raman (OHS-P01)** leads a source-stipulated **USD34B** healthcare group with **126,000staff across19countries**, headquartered in Singapore/Boston.
Network:86hospitals,440clinics,670diagnostic centres. Revenue mix:acute/specialty38%, primary/preventive22%, diagnostics18%, digital/pharmacy12%, research/value-based10%.
Source problems concern fragmented data, manual handoffs, delayed insight and privacy/cyber risk.
[Enterprise theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/06-health-care/omnihealth-systems-ltd/master/theme-a-enterprise.md)
## Act II — Choose the Future (OHS-M05–08)
**Dr.Leila Morgan (OHS-P03)** protects professional authority; experience director **Elena Brooks (OHS-P08)** raises access/equity; CFO **Michael Chen (OHS-P02)** requires credible value.
Compare facility-local pilots, enterprise platform first and federated care-journey experiments under common identity, safety, privacy and interoperability controls. Third option is provisional.
NPS\>65,25%lower documentation burden and15%operating-margin improvement are source ambitions with missing baselines/definitions.

## Act III — Architect Coordinated Care (OHS-M09–12)
Source chain: **Prevent → Discover → Diagnose → Treat → Recover → Monitor → Thrive**.
Identity lead **Priya Nair (OHS-P09)** and architect **Daniel Weber (OHS-P13)** connect Patient/Provider/Episode/Asset/Outcome360, workflow ownership and source-proposed EHR, LIMS, RIS/PACS, care management and FHIR-aligned integration.
**Omar Haddad (OHS-P10)** challenges unnecessary access. Standards-led proposals are not tested conformance. AI support remains subject to licensed professional judgment and evidence review.
[Architecture theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/06-health-care/omnihealth-systems-ltd/master/theme-c-architecture.md)
## Act IV — Fund and Sequence Evidence (OHS-M13–16)
Source proposes **USD620M over3years** and **USD250M annual value**. Cost breakdown, phasing, capacity-to-cash conversion, overlap and discounting are absent; NPV/payback are unvalidated.
Year1 identity/interoperability/cyber/access → Year2 command centre, virtual care and reviewed AI pilots → Year3 population health/personalized services.
Source targets:Year1 95%record match/20%digital appointments; Year2 15%lower wait/10%fewer avoidable readmissions; Year3 30%virtual follow-up/20%productivity improvement.
[Transformation theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/06-health-care/omnihealth-systems-ltd/master/theme-d-transformation.md)
## Act V — Make Change Accountable and Usable (OHS-M17–19)
Care coordination lead **Dr.Amina Okafor (OHS-P07)** asks who owns the next step. CHRO **Nina Fernandes (OHS-P06)** asks whether work is reduced or shifted. Operations lead **Dr.Arjun Mehta (OHS-P04)** requires reliable status and escalation.
Illustrative first pilot is bounded non-clinical scheduling/referral coordination with synthetic/de-identified approved test data. Test identity ambiguity, access, late events, downtime, reconciliation and rollback. No clinical treatment actions are included.
[Execution theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/06-health-care/omnihealth-systems-ltd/master/theme-e-execution.md)
## Act VI — Prove Value (OHS-M20–22)
Patient experience, access, quality, readmissions, clinician burden, productivity and cyber resilience need metric definitions, owners, baselines and balancing measures. A95%match rate alone does not establish identity safety.
All future figures are targets, not results. Scale, redesign or stop based on evidence.
[Value theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/06-health-care/omnihealth-systems-ltd/master/theme-f-value.md)
