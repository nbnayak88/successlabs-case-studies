# Hero Story — The Application That Could Not Move

> **GICS07 · Financials · Fictional educational case · v1.0 · 2026-10-19.** Enterprise figures are source-stipulated baselines or targets. New names, incidents, options and exercises are illustrative. This is enterprise-architecture learning, not financial advice, regulatory assurance or evidence of implemented outcomes.

## Prologue
In an invented SME onboarding exercise, a customer submits a document again because verification and service teams cannot agree on exception status. The story prompts discovery; no real incident or causal diagnosis is asserted.

## Act I — Understand
CEO Anika Shah asks how a58M-customer group can deliver trusted service across32countries while customer, risk, product and transaction evidence remain fragmented. (OCF-M01–04)

## Act II — Choose
Customer chief Priya Rao values inclusion; risk chief Arjun Mehta demands accountability; CFO David Morgan challenges the business case. Compare local pilots, platform first and federated journey experiments. (OCF-M05–08)

## Act III — Architect
Identity lead Mei Tan and architect Daniel Weber trace workflow, ownership, data contracts and reconciliation. CISO Omar Haddad challenges recovery assumptions; model-risk lead Dr.Leila Costa asks for evidence before high-impact use. (OCF-M09–12)

## Act IV — Fund learning

## Act V — Make change accountable
Compliance lead Elena Fischer asks who owns an unresolved exception; CHRO Nadia Fernandes distinguishes workforce onboarding from customer onboarding. A synthetic-data pilot tests permissions, evidence, late events, recovery and rollback without releasing high-impact decisions. (OCF-M17–19)
