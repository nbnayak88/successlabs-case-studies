# Theme A — Understand the Enterprise

> **GICS04 · Fictional educational case · v1.0 · 2026-10-19.** Source figures are fictional enterprise baselines. Names, incidents, strategic options and non-workforce designs are illustrative learning extensions. No implemented client outcome, selected product capability or approved budget is claimed.

## OVC-M01 — Enterprise Identity & Industry Context

Founded 1992; headquarters London and Singapore; 48 countries, USD 28B annual revenue, 72,000 employees and 64M stated active customers/loyalty members/digital shoppers. Count definitions and franchise coverage need validation.

**Learning deliverable:** Enterprise profile and scope register.

**Decision challenge:** What is the reporting boundary for employees, franchisees and active customers?

**Source locator:** Company Overview; Company Profile. [Evidence register](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).

## OVC-M02 — Business Model & Economic Engine

Fashion/lifestyle 32%, beauty/personal care 18%, home/living 17%, specialty retail 15%, travel/experiences 10%, digital marketplace 8%; sum 100%. Brand desirability, service, stock availability and workforce readiness are plausible margin drivers, not quantified findings.

**Learning deliverable:** Segment model and margin-driver hypothesis tree.

**Decision challenge:** How do marketplace economics differ from owned-store sales?

**Source locator:** Business Portfolio; Transformation Drivers. [Evidence register](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).

## OVC-M03 — Enterprise Ecosystem & Stakeholders

Source: 3,200 owned/franchise stores, 14 local e-commerce sites, 26 DCs, 140 micro-fulfillment locations and 3,800 suppliers/manufacturing partners. Include customers, employees, store/service leaders, HR/payroll, EA, suppliers and franchise partners.

**Learning deliverable:** Stakeholder and partner decision-rights map.

**Decision challenge:** Who owns a failed click-and-collect promise across a franchise and a shared platform?

**Source locator:** Strategic Assets; Business Context & Stakeholders. [Evidence register](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).

## OVC-M04 — Current-State Performance & Problem Diagnosis

Source problems concern country/brand workforce variation, fragmented employee/position/skills data, disconnected talent journeys, delayed reporting, multiple portals and legacy payroll/time dependencies. Customer inventory/returns failures are illustrative discovery triggers, not observed source incidents.

**Learning deliverable:** Issue tree separating source facts from hypotheses.

**Decision challenge:** Which process failure is evidenced, and which still needs discovery?

**Source locator:** Current-State Challenges. [Evidence register](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).
