Source capabilities: onboarding, credit/underwriting, financial crime, advice/service. Add supporting identity, consent, evidence, resilience and model governance as explicit design work. No numeric maturity baseline supplied.

**Learning deliverable:** Capability map and maturity evidence plan.

**Decision challenge:** Which capability is missing even if document AI is available?

**Source locator:** Phase B: Business Architecture. [Evidence](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).

## OCF-M10 — Value Streams & Process Architecture

Source chain: Acquire→Verify→Advise→Transact→Protect→Grow→Retain. Illustrative transformation streams: Identity-to-Accountable-Onboarding, Alert-to-Investigation, Consent-to-Partner-Service and Decision-to-Explainable-Outcome. Do not confuse customer onboarding with employee onboarding.

**Learning deliverable:** Stage/capability/process map.

**Decision challenge:** Where is an exception handed off, and how is ownership retained?

**Source locator:** Financial Value Stream; HR capability example. [Evidence](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).

## OCF-M11 — Target Operating Model & Organization

Source governed financial model links customer fairness, controls, data, digital journeys, resilience, model governance and value. Illustrative TOM: journey owners with common guardrails, accountable risk/control functions and legal-entity execution.

**Learning deliverable:** TOM, RACI and exception governance.

**Decision challenge:** Who may approve a release, accept residual risk or stop a service?

**Source locator:** 14.3;14.6. [Evidence](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).
