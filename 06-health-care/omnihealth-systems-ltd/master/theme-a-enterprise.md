# Theme A — Understand the Enterprise

> **GICS06 · Health Care · Fictional educational case · v1.0 · 2026-10-19.** Enterprise figures are source-stipulated baselines or targets. New names, incidents, options and exercises are illustrative. This case is architecture learning, not clinical guidance or evidence of a deployed clinical system.

## OHS-M01 — Enterprise Identity & Industry Context

Founded 1988; Singapore/Boston headquarters; 19 countries, USD 34B revenue and 126,000 clinicians, researchers and support staff. Source network: 86 hospitals, 440 clinics and 670 diagnostic centres. Define legal, care and workforce reporting boundaries.

**Learning deliverable:** Enterprise profile and scope register.

**Decision challenge:** Which care entities, partner services and staff are included?

**Source locator:** Company Overview; Company Profile. [Evidence](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).

## OHS-M02 — Business Model & Economic Engine

Acute/specialty care38%, primary/preventive22%, diagnostics18%, digital health/pharmacy12%, research/value-based care10%; sum100%. Revenue mix is not margin mix. Capacity, access, quality, administrative burden and payment models need distinct value hypotheses.

**Learning deliverable:** Segment economics and value-driver tree.

**Decision challenge:** Does increased utilization improve outcomes and contribution, or merely activity?

**Source locator:** Business Portfolio; Transformation Drivers. [Evidence](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).

## OHS-M03 — Enterprise Ecosystem & Stakeholders

Patients/families, clinicians, care coordinators, facilities, diagnostics, pharmacy, health plans, research partners, regulators and technology teams. Source promises coordinated care; decision rights and escalation ownership must be designed.

**Learning deliverable:** Stakeholder and decision-rights map.

**Decision challenge:** Who owns a referral that crosses a facility and payer boundary?

**Source locator:** Stakeholder Outcomes; Care Network. [Evidence](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).

## OHS-M04 — Current-State Performance & Problem Diagnosis

Source reports fragmented records/images/care plans, manual appointment/referral/discharge/billing handoffs, delayed insight and growing privacy/cyber exposure. No incident rates, measured wait-time baselines or quantified documentation baseline are supplied.

**Learning deliverable:** Issue tree and baseline evidence requests.

**Decision challenge:** Which handoff failure is evidenced and which is still a hypothesis?

**Source locator:** Current-State Challenges. [Evidence](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).
