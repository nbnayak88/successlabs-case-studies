## Prologue — A disconnected promise
An invented customer arrives for a collection the app marked ready. The store cannot reconcile the promise; a new associate is also waiting for access and training. These events are discovery triggers, not historical evidence or a proven causal diagnosis.
## Act I — Understand the Enterprise (OVC-M01–04)
Fictional CEO **Maya Sterling (OVC-P01)** leads a source-stipulated **USD 28B** consumer group with **72,000 employees across 48 countries**, headquartered in London and Singapore.
Source revenue mix: fashion/lifestyle 32%, beauty 18%, home 17%, specialty retail 15%, travel/experiences 10%, digital marketplace 8%. Source assets include3,200 owned/franchise stores,14 e-commerce sites,26 DCs,140 micro-fulfillment locations and3,800 suppliers/partners.
Most source architecture detail addresses fragmented workforce data, journeys, portals and integrations. Customer/fulfillment incidents and designs below are labelled extensions.
[Enterprise theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/04-consumer-discretionary/omnivibe-consumer-ltd/master/theme-a-enterprise.md)
## Act II — Choose the Future (OVC-M05–08)
Brand chief **Elena Rossi (OVC-P03)** values distinct experiences. CFO **Adrian Tan (OVC-P02)** demands evidence before funding. CHRO **Ravi Kapoor (OVC-P04)** connects workforce readiness to business outcomes.
Compare brand-local pilots, central platform first and federated journey experiments with common data/privacy/integration standards. The third is provisional.
**No investment budget, financial return or quantified KPI targets are supplied.** These remain TBD.

## Act III — Architect One Connected Workforce (OVC-M09–12)
The source 13 journeys connect foundation, acquisition, onboarding, performance, development, rewards, learning, payroll, time, analytics, experience, AI and integration.
Architect **Priya Shah (OVC-P11)** and integration lead **Jonas Lim (OVC-P12)** trace capabilities and data contracts. **Farah Ahmed (OVC-P13)** challenges unjustified data sharing.
Source SAP proposals include SuccessFactors modules, Work Zone, People Analytics, Joule and BTP/Integration Suite. Current capability, licensing and localization require verification. Illustrative consumer streams include Discover-to-Purchase, Promise-to-Fulfill, Return-to-Resolution, Trend-to-Assortment and Skill-to-Service-Readiness.
