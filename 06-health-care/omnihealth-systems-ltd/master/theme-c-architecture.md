# Theme C — Architect the Future Enterprise

> **GICS06 · Health Care · Fictional educational case · v1.0 · 2026-10-19.** Enterprise figures are source-stipulated baselines or targets. New names, incidents, options and exercises are illustrative. This case is architecture learning, not clinical guidance or evidence of a deployed clinical system.

## OHS-M09 — Business Capability Architecture

Source capabilities: care navigation, clinical operations, population health and revenue cycle. Define maturity and supporting identity, consent, scheduling, capacity and evidence capabilities. No numeric maturity baseline is supplied.

**Learning deliverable:** Capability map and maturity evidence plan.

**Decision challenge:** Which missing capability blocks safe information sharing despite an available API?

**Source locator:** Phase B: Business Architecture. [Evidence](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).

## OHS-M10 — Value Streams & Process Architecture

Preserve source chain Prevent→Discover→Diagnose→Treat→Recover→Monitor→Thrive. Source blueprint shortens this by omitting Discover; retain both as views pending vocabulary reconciliation. Illustrative streams: Referral-to-Coordinated-Care, Episode-to-Follow-up, Capacity-to-Accessible-Service and Evidence-to-Outcome.

**Learning deliverable:** Stage/capability/process map with handoff ownership.

**Decision challenge:** How will ownership persist across referral, discharge and follow-up?

**Source locator:** Care Value Stream;14.3. [Evidence](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).

## OHS-M11 — Target Operating Model & Organization

Source proposes governed care operating model linking safety, workflow, data, interoperability, privacy, adoption and outcomes. Illustrative TOM: common guardrails with facility clinical authority, journey owners, privacy stewards and operational teams.

**Learning deliverable:** TOM, RACI and escalation model.

**Decision challenge:** Who may approve a workflow change and who may stop it?

**Source locator:** 14.3;14.6. [Evidence](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).

## OHS-M12 — Enterprise Architecture & Digital Enablement

Source Patient/Provider/CareEpisode/Asset/Outcome360; EHR, LIMS, RIS/PACS, ERP, CRM, care management; FHIR-aligned gateway/APIs, iPaaS and events; governed data/image platforms; hybrid cloud and edge. These are conceptual source proposals, not validated conformance or product selections.

**Learning deliverable:** Conceptual architecture, identity lineage and trust boundaries.

**Decision challenge:** How will ambiguous identity or stale data be surfaced rather than hidden?

**Source locator:** Phase C; Phase D. [Evidence](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).
