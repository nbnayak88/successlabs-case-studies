# OmniGrid — Executive One-Page Brief

> **GICS10 · Utilities · Fictional educational case · v1.0 · 2026-10-19.** Source figures are fictional enterprise baselines or targets. New names, incidents, options and exercises are illustrative. No production-grid action, approved investment, regulatory assurance or achieved outcome is asserted.

**Decision:** Fund bounded outage/work-order/customer coordination evidence before wider modernization.

**Situation:** USD31B utility;38,000staff;17countries;21M electricity/gas/water accounts. Renewables28%, networks27%, retail18%, conventional/flexible15%, water/environment12%.

**Source problem:** Fragmented OT/field/customer/market data, variable generation, manual restoration and growing meter/sensor security demands.

**Illustrative trigger:** A storm scenario generates conflicting outage, asset and work-order status. Field and customer teams cannot reconcile ownership. This is invented learning context, not an actual incident.

**Options:** Local asset/region pilots; central platform/grid first; federated coordination pilots with shared data, safety, OT-security and evidence standards. Third option provisional.

**Architecture:** Forecast→Generate→Transmit→Distribute→Consume→Flex→Restore. Asset/Grid/Customer/Energy/WorkOrder360; ADMS/SCADA/EAM/OMS/CIS/billing/market systems. Source chain is electricity-centric; water/gas workflows need discovery. Local operational authority constrains automation.

**Economics:** ProposedUSD970M over3years; USD360Mannual value assumption. Capital deferral, operating savings and revenue need different timing/cash treatment. Split, cost, overlap and discounting absent; no validated NPV/payback.
