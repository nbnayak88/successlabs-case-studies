# OmniHealth — Executive One-Page Brief

> **GICS06 · Health Care · Fictional educational case · v1.0 · 2026-10-19.** Enterprise figures are source-stipulated baselines or targets. New names, incidents, options and exercises are illustrative. This case is architecture learning, not clinical guidance or evidence of a deployed clinical system.

**Decision:** Select a bounded, non-clinical care-coordination pilot and evidence gates before enterprise expansion.

**Situation:** USD34B healthcare group;126,000staff;19countries;86hospitals,440clinics,670diagnostic centres. Acute38%, primary22%, diagnostics18%, digital/pharmacy12%, research/value-based10%.

**Source problem:** Fragmented patient/diagnostic/care-plan data; manual appointment, referral, discharge and billing handoffs; limited timely insight; privacy/cyber exposure.

**Illustrative trigger:** A referral crosses facilities without clear ownership; the patient repeats information and an operational dashboard cannot reconcile episode status. No actual patient incident is asserted.

**Choice:** Local facility pilots; platform-first centralization; federated journey pilots with common identity, privacy, interoperability and governance. Third option is provisional.

**Architecture:** Prevent→Discover→Diagnose→Treat→Recover→Monitor→Thrive. Source proposes Patient/Provider/Episode/Asset/Outcome360, EHR/LIMS/RIS-PACS, care management, FHIR-aligned integration, hybrid cloud and edge. Conformance is unverified; licensed professional judgment remains authoritative.

**Economics:** ProposedUSD620M over3years; USD250M annual value assumption. Category split, cash conversion, recurring cost, overlap and discounting absent; no validated NPV/payback.

|Source target|Measure requiring definition|
|---|---|
|Year1:95%record-match rate;20%digital appointments|Identity errors/cohorts; appointment denominator|
|Year2:15%lower wait;10%fewer avoidable readmissions|Baseline, cohort, attribution, balancing measures|
|Year3:30%virtual follow-up;20%productivity improvement|Eligibility, equity/workload and method|
|Other:NPS>65;25%less documentation;15%margin improvement|Baseline, timeframe and calculation|

**Gates:** Workflow owner, identity/access/privacy evidence, professional review boundaries, usability, downtime/recovery and finance assurance. All future values are targets, not achieved outcomes.
