## OMF-M15 — Transformation Roadmap & Dependencies

Year 1 foundation: governance, OT connectivity, asset pilots, ESG baseline (95% master-data quality;20% assets connected targets). Year 2 acceleration: lakehouse, priority AI and control tower (10 models;10% downtime reduction target). Year 3: digital twins/circularity/optimization pilots;25% carbon-intensity reduction and 75% digitization targets. Define whether downtime changes are relative or percentage points.

**Learning deliverable:** 36-month dependency roadmap and stage gates

**Decision challenge:** Why must genealogy and laboratory reconciliation precede customer-facing claims?

**Source locator:** 3-Year Implementation Timeline. [Evidence register](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).

## OMF-M16 — Transformation Governance & Delivery Model

CEO steering with finance assurance, architecture review, plant safety authority, cyber approval, sustainability evidence and workforce adoption. Funding released by evidence gates rather than a single platform milestone.

**Learning deliverable:** Governance charter, decision log and exceptions process

**Decision challenge:** Who may accept residual risk, and who must remain able to veto an unsafe release?

**Source locator:** Leadership & Governance;14.6. [Evidence register](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).
