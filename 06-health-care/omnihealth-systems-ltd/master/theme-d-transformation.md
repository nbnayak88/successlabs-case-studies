# Theme D — Design the Transformation

> **GICS06 · Health Care · Fictional educational case · v1.0 · 2026-10-19.** Enterprise figures are source-stipulated baselines or targets. New names, incidents, options and exercises are illustrative. This case is architecture learning, not clinical guidance or evidence of a deployed clinical system.

## OHS-M13 — Transformation Initiative & Innovation Portfolio

Source candidates: deterioration alerts with clinician review, imaging triage with radiologist oversight, capacity command centre and grounded patient-service copilot. Sequence administrative/access experiments and evidence readiness before any high-impact deployment; no clinical model validation is supplied.

**Learning deliverable:** Initiative register and bounded experiment canvas.

**Decision challenge:** Which administrative problem can be tested without automating clinical judgment?

**Source locator:** AI & Advanced Analytics;14.4. [Evidence](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).

## OHS-M14 — Business Case & Investment Economics

Source proposes USD620M over3years andUSD250M annual value across capacity, variation, administration and quality. Category split, phased cash flows, recurring costs, overlap, discount rate and financial valuation of clinical outcomes are absent. No validated ROI, NPV or payback.

**Learning deliverable:** Cost/benefit assumptions, cash flows and downside case.

**Decision challenge:** How much released capacity becomes usable service or cash, and at what additional cost?

**Source locator:** Investment & Outcomes. [Evidence](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).

## OHS-M15 — Transformation Roadmap & Dependencies

Year1 identity/interoperability/cyber/access:95% record-match rate,20% digital appointments. Year2 command centre/virtual care/AI/pathways:15% lower wait,10% fewer avoidable readmissions. Year3 population health/personalization:30% virtual follow-up,20% productivity improvement. All are targets with undefined populations and measures.

**Learning deliverable:** 36-month dependency map and release gates.

**Decision challenge:** What evidence must precede expansion to another facility or patient cohort?

**Source locator:** 3-Year Roadmap & Value Realisation. [Evidence](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).

## OHS-M16 — Transformation Governance & Delivery Model

Source requires clinical governance, privacy and human oversight. Illustrative steering combines clinical safety, operations, patient representation, finance, data, architecture, security and research. Record decisions, local constraints and approved use boundaries.

**Learning deliverable:** Governance charter, safety review and decision log.

**Decision challenge:** Who can halt a model or service when workflow or performance changes?

**Source locator:** 14.3;14.6; Responsible AI rule. [Evidence](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).
