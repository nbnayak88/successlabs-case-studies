## OMF-SIM-01 — CEO discovery
- **Counterparts:** OMF-P01, OMF-P03, OMF-P04.
- **Master references:** OMF-M01, OMF-M03, OMF-M04.

- **Opening objection:** “Why should I fund another transformation?”
- **Withheld constraint:** The customer certificate mismatch is an invented trigger, not a diagnosed root cause. Reveal after relevant questioning.
- **Follow-up challenge:** Ask for the customer impact, process owner and source evidence.
