## Learning flow
Read and question the case, simulate stakeholder decisions, decode capabilities and value stages, defend architecture decisions, run a reversible experiment with synthetic data, then record evidence and reflect. Blank evidence remains incomplete. Failed tests or missing evidence hold the workbook's release recommendation. Human approval remains required.

The optional supporting-proportion calculator applies only to counts with a valid matching population. Time-to-fill, time-to-competence and forecasting error require their own appropriate measurement method. A percentage is not substituted for these domain KPIs.
