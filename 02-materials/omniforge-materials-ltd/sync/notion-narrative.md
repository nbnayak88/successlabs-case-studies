## Prologue — A shipment with three versions of the truth
A fictional industrial customer queries a shipped batch's certificate after a laboratory correction fails to reach the customer portal. At a plant, a critical-asset warning arrives while procurement cannot reconcile a recycled-feedstock record. These invented events expose a discovery question: can OmniForge trust its material, quality and carbon evidence from extraction through recovery?

## Act I — Understand the Enterprise (OMF-M01–04)
Fictional CEO **Ananya Deshpande (OMF-P01)** leads a USD42 B materials enterprise with 46,000 employees across 31 countries, headquartered in Mumbai and Rotterdam. Its revenue mix is steel/alloys 30%, cement/aggregates 22%, chemicals 20%, mining/minerals 16% and advanced materials 12%.
The source lists 54 production sites,27 mines/quarries and 18 cement plants; these counts may overlap. Fragmented planning, inconsistent master data,180+applications and isolated operational data make reliable decisions difficult.
**Discovery challenge:** Which broken handoff creates the greatest customer, safety or value risk?
[Read the Enterprise theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/02-materials/omniforge-materials-ltd/master/theme-a-enterprise.md)

## Act II — Choose the Future (OMF-M05–08)
CFO **Thomas van Dijk (OMF-P02)** demands financial discipline. COO **Kavita Rao (OMF-P03)** protects operational continuity. Chief Digital and Sustainability Officer **Sofia de Vries (OMF-P04)** requires auditable material and carbon evidence.
Compare three illustrative options: local pilots, enterprise platform first, and federated value-stream pilots under common safety/data standards. The third is provisional and must survive evidence gates.
The source's 35%Scope 1/2 emissions reduction by 2032 differs from a Year 3 carbon-intensity index 100→75. Both remain targets with distinct boundaries.
[Read the Strategy theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/02-materials/omniforge-materials-ltd/master/theme-b-strategy.md)

## Act III — Architect the Digital Thread (OMF-M09–12)
The source value chain is **Explore → Extract → Process → Manufacture → Distribute → Recover → Reuse**. Illustrative transformation streams connect Feedstock-to-Qualified-Material, Asset-to-Reliability, Order-to-Trusted-Delivery, Recover-to-Reuse and Evidence-to-Carbon-Claim.
Architect **Priya Nair (OMF-P13)** connects capabilities and process owners to ERP, MES, EAM, LIMS, MDM, edge, APIs and events. Quality lead **Rohan Kulkarni (OMF-P08)** demands traceable lab corrections and batch genealogy. Data/AI lead **Diego Alvarez (OMF-P14)** requires ownership and lineage; CISO **Hassan Al-Farsi (OMF-P12)** insists critical controls remain within safe plant boundaries.
[Read the Architecture theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/02-materials/omniforge-materials-ltd/master/theme-c-architecture.md)

## Act IV — Fund Learning Before Scale (OMF-M13–16)
The fictional source proposes **USD540 M over 36 months**: operations/OT230 M, data/AI125 M, supply-chain/CX90 M, cyber 55 M and change 40 M.
Listed benefit categories total **USD350 M**, but 65 M working capital may represent cash release rather than recurring savings. Predictive-maintenance 70 M may overlap efficiency 190 M. No validated NPV or payback is provided.
Year 1 establishes foundations and pilots; Year 2 integrates data/AI/planning; Year 3 extends twins, circularity and controlled optimization. Safety, quality, workforce and financial gates govern scale.
[Read the Transformation theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/02-materials/omniforge-materials-ltd/master/theme-d-transformation.md)

## Act V — Make Change Safe and Human (OMF-M17–19)
Plant Director **Elena Petrova (OMF-P07)** requires safe operating limits and rollback. CHRO **Arjun Mehta (OMF-P05)** and shift representative **Nikhil Sen (OMF-P16)** challenge learning that excludes shifts, contractors or people without devices. Customer **Amelia Chen (OMF-P15)** expects certificate correction notifications with accountable owners.
A bounded pilot must test genealogy, laboratory corrections, outage behavior, access controls, drift and human decisions before any controlled expansion.
[Read the Execution theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/02-materials/omniforge-materials-ltd/master/theme-e-execution.md)

## Act VI — Prove or Change the Thesis (OMF-M20–22)
Source baseline→Year 3 targets are **OEE68%→83%, downtime 9%→3%, first-pass yield 89%→97%, carbon-intensity index 100→75, recycled content 18%→40%, automation 20%→75%**. None is an achieved result.
The board decides to scale, redesign or stop using observed evidence. The 2030 vision remains aspirational.
[Read the Value theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/02-materials/omniforge-materials-ltd/master/theme-f-value.md)
