## Prologue — A coordination gap
An invented storm exercise creates conflicting outage, asset and work-order status. Field and customer teams cannot reconcile next ownership. This prompts discovery, not a claim of an actual incident orproven cause.

## Act I — Understand the Enterprise (OGU-M01–04)
Fictional CEO **Anika Sørensen (OGU-P01)** leads a source-stipulated **USD31B** utility with **38,000staff,17countries and21M electricity/gas/water accounts**, headquartered in Copenhagen/Sydney. Accounts do not necessarily equal unique people.
Mix:renewables28%, networks27%, retail18%, conventional/flexible15%, water/environment12%.
Source explicitly identifies **Utilities/GICS10**, resolving the request's Communication Services wording.
[Enterprise theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/10-utilities/omnigrid-utilities-ltd/master/theme-a-enterprise.md)

## Act II — Choose the Future (OGU-M05–08)
Operations chief **Arjun Mehta (OGU-P04)** requires local authority; customer chief **Maya Patel (OGU-P03)** asks about affordability/participation; CFO **David Chen (OGU-P02)** challenges economics.
Compare local asset/region pilots, central platform/grid first and federated coordination experiments under common guardrails. Third option provisional.
Source99.99%reliability,30%faster restoration and15%capital-productivity improvement lack sufficient scope/baselines.
[Strategy theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/10-utilities/omnigrid-utilities-ltd/master/theme-b-strategy.md)

## Act III — Architect Trusted Coordination (OGU-M09–12)
Source chain: **Forecast → Generate → Transmit → Distribute → Consume → Flex → Restore**. It is electricity-centric; water/gas workflows require discovery.
**Mei Lin (OGU-P09)** and **Daniel Weber (OGU-P12)** connect Asset/Grid/Customer/Energy/WorkOrder360, ADMS/SCADA/EAM/OMS/CIS/billing/market systems and governed industrial/API/event contracts.
**Omar Haddad (OGU-P10)** requires permissions, segmentation, monitoring and recoverability. Source edge/hybrid/self-healing proposals are not validated operating controls.
[Architecture theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/10-utilities/omnigrid-utilities-ltd/master/theme-c-architecture.md)

## Act IV — Fund and Sequence Evidence (OGU-M13–16)
Source proposes **USD970M over3years** and **USD360M annual value**. Deferred capital, operating savings, loss reduction and flexible revenue need distinct cash/timing treatment. Split, run cost, overlap and discounting absent; NPV/payback unvalidated.
Year1 asset data, OT security, grid-data platform → Year2 ADMS, maintenance, field mobility → Year3 DER orchestration, self-healing pilots and customer flexibility.
Targets:95%critical records complete;20%faster restoration/15%fewer failures;30%flex participation/25%lower losses.
[Transformation theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/10-utilities/omnigrid-utilities-ltd/master/theme-d-transformation.md)

## Act V — Make Change Usable and Accountable (OGU-M17–19)
Field lead **Marcus Okoro (OGU-P08)** asks who owns the next work step. CHRO **Nadia Fernandes (OGU-P07)** requires role/shift practice and support.
Illustrative first pilot uses synthetic data and simulated events to test identifiers, topology freshness, permissions, late events, work ownership, reconciliation, offline behavior and rollback. No live grid, water orplant action executed.
[Execution theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/10-utilities/omnigrid-utilities-ltd/master/theme-e-execution.md)

## Act VI — Prove Community Value (OGU-M20–22)
SAIDI/SAIFI, restoration, curtailment, asset failures, losses, satisfaction and carbon measures require baselines, owners, cohorts and balancing measures.
**30%restoration ambition versus20%Year2target** remains a scope/time/definition gap. Completeness is not accuracy; carbon intensity is not absolute emissions. All future figures are targets, not results.
[Value theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/10-utilities/omnigrid-utilities-ltd/master/theme-f-value.md)
