# OmniCapital — Executive One-Page Brief

> **GICS07 · Financials · Fictional educational case · v1.0 · 2026-10-19.** Enterprise figures are source-stipulated baselines or targets. New names, incidents, options and exercises are illustrative. This is enterprise-architecture learning, not financial advice, regulatory assurance or evidence of implemented outcomes.

**Decision:** Fund a bounded onboarding/document-exception pilot with control and value evidence before enterprise expansion.

**Situation:** USD46B financial group; USD1.4T AUM;58Mcustomers;32countries. Retail31%, commercial24%, wealth19%, insurance16%, payments/markets10%. Staff count not supplied.

**Source problem:** Fragmented customer/product/risk/transaction data, manual review, repeated entry, legacy constraints and stronger fraud/cyber/model-risk control needs.

**Illustrative trigger:** An SME customer repeatedly submits the same document while customer-service and verification teams show different exception status. This is invented discovery context, not an actual customer incident.

**Options:** Local product pilots; core/platform first; federated journey experiments with common identity, data, control and resilience standards. Third option is provisional.

**Architecture:** Acquire→Verify→Advise→Transact→Protect→Grow→Retain. Source Customer/Account/Transaction/Risk/Product360, domain services, open APIs, events and governed data. Human accountability and model evidence constrain high-impact decisions.

**Economics:** USD780M proposed3-year investment; USD340Mannual value assumption. Split, phasing, incremental costs, overlap, cash conversion and discount rate absent. No validated NPV/payback.
