## Cross-cutting mandatory metadata
Every section must identify: scenario facts vs assumptions, relevant persona owners, capabilities, strategic objectives, value-stream stages, initiatives, KPIs, decisions, risks, evidence status, and review date.

## Reuse contract
Derived streams link to stable master section IDs **OW-M01 through OW-M22**. No independent redefinition of canonical figures. For role-play, cite the corresponding persona IDs and scenario IDs.
