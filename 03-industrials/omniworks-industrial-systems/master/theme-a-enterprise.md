# Theme A — Understand the Enterprise

> **Fictional OmniWorks case study.** Baselines are scenario facts; proposed designs, timelines and benefits are illustrative until validated.

## OW-M01 — Enterprise Identity & Industry Context

OmniWorks is a fictional global industrial equipment and lifecycle-services enterprise: USD 48B revenue, 74,000 employees, 36 countries, 61 manufacturing/engineering/assembly facilities, 320 service depots and 7,200 suppliers. Treat these as scenario baselines, not verified industry benchmarks.

**Required consulting artifact:** Enterprise fact sheet; GICS03 profile; industrial footprint and scope glossary.

**Discovery prompt:** What evidence, decision owner, constraints and measurable acceptance criteria would make this section credible?

## OW-M02 — Business Model & Economic Engine

Five revenue segments: automation 27%, aerospace and defence 23%, mobility 19%, infrastructure 16%, aftermarket and lifecycle services 15%. Shift from product sales toward connected services, uptime contracts and installed-base monetization.

**Discovery prompt:** What evidence, decision owner, constraints and measurable acceptance criteria would make this section credible?
