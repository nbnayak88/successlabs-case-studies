# Hero Story — The Bundle That Was Only Half Alive

> **GICS09 · Communication Services · Fictional educational case · v1.0 · 2026-10-19.** Source figures are fictional enterprise baselines or targets. New names, incidents, options and exercises are illustrative. No approved investment, tested production network, standards conformance or achieved outcome is asserted.

## Prologue
An invented converged offer is billed as active, but connectivity and content entitlements are incomplete. Care, network and billing teams show different states. The story prompts discovery, not a claim of an actual outage or diagnosed cause.

## Act I — Understand
CEO Anika Rahman asks how a210M-subscription group can connect networks, content and trusted experiences across28countries while lifecycle domains remain separate. (OCN-M01–04)

## Act II — Choose
Convergence chief Maya Patel demands simplicity; network chief Arjun Mehta protects operational authority; CFO David Chen challenges value. Compare local improvements, central platform first and federated converged-service pilots. (OCN-M05–08)

## Act III — Architect
Fulfillment lead Priya Shah and architect Daniel Weber trace catalog, order, service, network, billing and rights state. Consent lead Mei Lin and CISO Omar Haddad define permitted use and recoverable boundaries. (OCN-M09–12)

## Act IV — Fund learning
Source proposes1.1B over3years and410Mannual value. Finance needs run cost, retention-to-margin, phasing and overlap evidence. Identity/network-data foundations precede controlled activation, assurance and API expansion. (OCN-M13–16)

## Act V — Prove service and recovery
Assurance lead Marcus Okoro demands subscriber-impact verification; CHRO Nadia Fernandes asks for field/care practice and support. A synthetic-data pilot simulates network actions, exceptions, stop/rollback and state reconciliation; no production changes executed. (OCN-M17–19)

## Act VI — Measure trust
Core availability is not full subscriber experience;30%activation and25%fulfillment targets need distinct definitions. Energy intensity is not absolute consumption. Leadership scales, redesigns or stops using evidence; no historical success invented. (OCN-M20–22)
