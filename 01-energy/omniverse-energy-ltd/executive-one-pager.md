# OmniVerse Energy Ltd. — Executive One-Page Case Study

**GICS01 · Energy | Board-level transformation brief | Fictional case v1.0**

> **Hero story:** After an invented refinery reliability alert, inconsistent customer communications and renewable dispatch uncertainty, fictional CEO **Dr. Asha Menon** must decide how to modernize a USD 85B integrated energy business while protecting safety, energy reliability, workforce trust and capital.

## Enterprise at a glance
| Profile | Fictional Notion baseline |
|---|---|
| Scale | USD 85B revenue; 68,000 employees; 42 countries |
| Business mix | Upstream 35%; refining/petrochemicals 28%; retail/marketing 18%; power 12%; renewables 7% |
| Assets | 8 refineries; 25,000 km pipelines; 12,500 retail sites; 15 GW generation; 5 GW operating renewables |
| Board ambition | Safe, connected operations; energy transition; customer trust; auditable value |

## The burning platform
Disconnected operational data, reactive maintenance, inconsistent customer journeys, fragmented ESG reporting and technology silos inhibit enterprise-wide decisions. The energy transition adds capital-allocation and regulatory complexity.

## Transformation thesis
**From siloed energy assets to a connected, resilient, customer-centric energy enterprise.**

| Pillar | Strategic move |
|---|---|
| Reliable energy operations | Asset 360, condition monitoring, predictive maintenance and safety-led OT |
| Integrated energy value streams | Explore-to-customer and generate-to-serve visibility |
| Trusted data and AI | Governed master data, data quality, lineage and accountable AI |
| Customer experience | Unified service journeys, digital channels and transparent communications |
| Energy transition | Measurable emissions, renewables integration and ESG traceability |
| People and governance | Skills, rewards, change adoption, portfolio governance and cyber assurance |

## Decision and execution
**Three options:** local pilots; central platform first; **federated value-stream delivery with shared standards** (provisional recommendation). **36-month sequence:** Year 1 foundations/pilots → Year 2 integration/scale → Year 3 optimization/outcome-led services.

## Investment and value (illustrative; not validated)
**Three-year investment: USD 900M.** Listed benefit categories total **USD 635M per year** at assumed run-rate, not proven net cash flow. NPV/payback cannot be accepted until phasing, discounting, recurring costs, benefit overlap and sensitivities are reconciled.

| KPI | Baseline | Year 3 target (not achieved) |
|---|---:|---:|
| OEE | 72% | 85% |
| Unplanned downtime | 8% | 2% |
| NPS | 35 | 65 |
| Digital channel adoption | 25% | 85% |
| Revenue from digital services | 2% | 15% |
