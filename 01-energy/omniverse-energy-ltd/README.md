# OmniVerse Energy Ltd. — Enterprise Business Transformation Case Study

> **GICS01 · Fictional educational case · v1.0 (2026-10-19).** Figures from the supplied Notion case are fictional scenario baselines or targets. New characters and incidents are illustrative. No real client or employee is represented.

## The story
A multinational energy conglomerate with a legacy oil-and-gas core and growing renewable ambitions faces a board-level dilemma: how can it reduce operational risk, modernize energy assets, improve customer trust and deliver a credible energy transition without sacrificing safety, profitability or workforce confidence?

Start with [Executive One-Pager](executive-one-pager.md), then read the [Hero Story](hero-story.md), meet the [16 Fictional Personas](personas/README.md), explore the [22-Section Master](master/README.md), and practice with [Yoodli-ready Simulations](simulations/README.md).
