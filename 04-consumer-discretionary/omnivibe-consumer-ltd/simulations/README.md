## Practice instructions
Select one scenario and counterpart. Ask the AI to play that fictional stakeholder, open with the stated objection, reveal withheld details only after discovery, challenge assumptions and debrief the expected artifact. Practice a 45-second answer, then a follow-up and reflection. This is practice guidance, not an official exam format. Live Yoodli sessions have not been created.
