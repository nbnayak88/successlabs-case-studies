# Theme B — Define Strategic Direction

> **GICS07 · Financials · Fictional educational case · v1.0 · 2026-10-19.** Enterprise figures are source-stipulated baselines or targets. New names, incidents, options and exercises are illustrative. This is enterprise-architecture learning, not financial advice, regulatory assurance or evidence of implemented outcomes.

## OCF-M05 — Market, Industry & Competitive Intelligence

Investigate customer expectations, cost-to-serve, open ecosystems, sustainable finance and risk transparency. These are source discovery themes, not independently researched market forecasts, laws or competitor benchmarks.

**Learning deliverable:** Customer/partner and market evidence plan.

**Decision challenge:** What customer evidence justifies a proposed embedded-finance use case?

**Source locator:** Transformation Drivers. [Evidence](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).

## OCF-M06 — Strategic Ambition & Transformation Vision

Source vision joins trusted, inclusive, intelligent experiences with secure platforms and responsible innovation. NPS>60, 90% digital service completion, 35% automation and 14% ROE improvement are unvalidated target statements with missing baselines/definitions.

**Learning deliverable:** North Star and outcome/constraint register.

**Decision challenge:** How will faster service remain fair, inclusive and accountable?

**Source locator:** Vision; Stakeholder Outcomes. [Evidence](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).

## OCF-M07 — Strategic Options & Investment Choices

Illustrative options: A product-local pilots; B enterprise core/platform first; C federated customer-journey pilots under common identity, data, control and resilience standards. C is provisional and requires evidence and approval.

**Learning deliverable:** Options matrix and decision evidence.

**Decision challenge:** What would make an exception necessary or stop a proposed journey experiment?

**Source locator:** 14.3 and14.6; illustrative extension. [Evidence](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).

## OCF-M08 — Transformation Thesis & Value Creation

Trusted customer/account/transaction/risk/product data plus controlled workflow should reduce repeated work, improve service and support accountable risk decisions. Test causal value, overlap and responsible ownership rather than equating automation with benefit.

**Learning deliverable:** Causal benefits tree and owners.

**Decision challenge:** Which released capacity becomes usable service or cash, and which shifts work?

**Source locator:** 14.3 Target operating model; Investment & Outcomes. [Evidence](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).
