## Case-study conventions

- Store each case study under the relevant sector in its own kebab-case folder.
- Document provider name, business line, business context, architecture, initiatives, and learning labs.
- Clearly label illustrative assumptions and fictional enterprises.
- Link from other SuccessLabs Academy domain repositories rather than duplicating case-study content.
