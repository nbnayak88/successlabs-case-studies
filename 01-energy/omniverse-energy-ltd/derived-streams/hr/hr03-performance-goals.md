### B — Direction (master 05–08)
**Strategic dilemma:** Should OmniEnergy use one enterprise performance model, separate business-unit models, or common principles with governed persona variants? Maya must balance simplicity, strategic alignment, evidence, fairness, coaching quality, and integration with learning, succession, and rewards.

**Discovery questions:** Which worker population and business decision are affected? Which source record proves the problem? Who approves country variants? What must remain safe during a failed handoff? What evidence would make us reject our preferred design?

**Options:** retain and control existing local practice; impose a uniform template; or use a common core with justified local variants. Compare worker impact, control coverage, delivery effort and reversibility. No option is approved merely by appearing here.

### C — Architecture (master 09–12)
**OVE-HR03-CAP01 capability group:** Goal alignment; coaching; calibration; appeals. These are abilities; the following sequence is a value stream, not a capability hierarchy.

**OVE-HR03-VS01 value stream:** Strategy → agree contribution → coach → evidence → calibrate → develop. Define a stakeholder-valued exit criterion at each stage before detailed process modeling.

**Information objects:** goal; evidence period; metric owner; review; calibration decision; appeal. Record attribute owner, authoritative source, purpose, access, retention, effective date and quality rule.

**Application responsibility:** Performance owns contribution and review evidence; HSE and ESG own validated operating facts; rewards consumes approved results, not raw manager assertions.

**Architecture decision OVE-HR03-ADR01 (proposed):** maintain explicit ownership and governed handoffs rather than uncontrolled copies. HR12 owns transport/recovery patterns; this domain owns its business rules and acceptance. Identity, data, UX and infrastructure views reference the shared [architecture boundaries](operating-standard.md).

### D — Transformation (master 13–16)
**OVE-HR03-REQ01:** every in-scope business change has a unique case/event reference, authorized decision, effective date and traceable downstream outcome. **OVE-HR03-REQ02:** a rejected or delayed change remains visible to an accountable owner; a silent drop cannot count as completion. Define domain-specific fields above before build.

**OVE-HR03-EXP01 — delivery experiment:** use synthetic data for one representative population and one contrasting workforce/country variant. Establish the baseline, replay the decision scenes below, compare outcomes and collect worker feedback. Progress: discovery → stakeholder simulation → capability/value-stream design → architecture decision → experiment → evidence/outcomes. Learners may loop back when evidence changes the question.

**Gates:** design owner accepts rules and source ownership; sponsor accepts business scope; payroll/HSE/security accept applicable controls; pilot owner demonstrates failure recovery; Finance accepts any value claim. Scale only after documented acceptance. Funding and delivery dates remain TBD; source timelines are learning assumptions.
