## Prologue — An exception without shared ownership
An invented SME customer repeatedly submits a document while service and verification teams show different exception status. This triggers discovery, not a claim about an actual customer or proven cause.

## Act I — Understand the Enterprise (OCF-M01–04)
Fictional CEO **Anika Shah (OCF-P01)** leads a source-stipulated **USD46B** group with **USD1.4T AUM,58M customers and32countries**, headquartered in London/Mumbai.
Revenue mix:retail/digital31%, commercial24%, wealth19%, insurance16%, payments/markets10%. AUM is not revenue, owned assets or cash. Staff and branch counts are not supplied.
Source problems:fragmented data, manual onboarding/service, legacy-core constraints and rising control demands.
[Enterprise theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/07-financials/omnicapital-financial-group-ltd/master/theme-a-enterprise.md)

## Act II — Choose the Future (OCF-M05–08)
Customer chief **Priya Rao (OCF-P03)** asks who is excluded; risk chief **Arjun Mehta (OCF-P04)** requires accountability; CFO **David Morgan (OCF-P02)** demands credible value.
Compare product-local pilots, enterprise platform first and federated customer-journey experiments under common controls. Third option is provisional.
Source ambitions NPS>60,90%digital completion,35%automation and14%ROE improvement lack sufficient definitions/baselines.
[Strategy theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/07-financials/omnicapital-financial-group-ltd/master/theme-b-strategy.md)

## Act III — Architect Trusted Financial Journeys (OCF-M09–12)
Source chain: **Acquire → Verify → Advise → Transact → Protect → Grow → Retain**.
Identity lead **Mei Tan (OCF-P09)** and architect **Daniel Weber (OCF-P12)** connect Customer/Account/Transaction/Risk/Product360 to source-proposed core banking, policy admin, CRM, lending, payments, risk engines, APIs and events.
**Omar Haddad (OCF-P10)** requires tested access/recovery;**Dr.Leila Costa (OCF-P13)** requires model purpose, fairness, explanation, monitoring and accountable review. Proposals are not validated systems or regulatory assurance.
[Architecture theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/07-financials/omnicapital-financial-group-ltd/master/theme-c-architecture.md)

## Act IV — Fund and Sequence Evidence (OCF-M13–16)
Source proposes **USD780M over3years** and **USD340M annual value**. Components, phasing, incremental costs, overlap, cash conversion and capital effects are absent; NPV/payback remain unvalidated.
Year1 MDM/API/cloud/identity/fraud → Year2 journey/risk-data/automation → Year3 embedded finance, advice and real-time risk proposals.
Targets:Year1 95%identity match/25%digital onboarding; Year2 40%faster onboarding/20%lower service cost; Year3 70%self-service/30%automation.
[Transformation theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/07-financials/omnicapital-financial-group-ltd/master/theme-d-transformation.md)

## Act V — Make Change Accountable and Usable (OCF-M17–19)
Compliance lead **Elena Fischer (OCF-P05)** asks who owns unresolved evidence. CHRO **Nadia Fernandes (OCF-P07)** preserves the source's employee-onboarding example while distinguishing it from customer onboarding.
Illustrative first pilot:synthetic-data document intake/exception routing with approved access, identity uncertainty, late/duplicate events, evidence lineage, reconciliation, recovery and rollback. No automated credit, pricing or advice release is included.
[Execution theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/07-financials/omnicapital-financial-group-ltd/master/theme-e-execution.md)

## Act VI — Prove Value (OCF-M20–22)
NPS, cost-to-income, fraud loss, onboarding time, model evidence and compliance require owners, baselines, definitions and balancing measures.
**35%versus30%automation** and **90%completion versus70%self-service** remain visible scope/time/definition gaps. Match rate alone does not prove identity assurance. All future figures are targets, not results.
[Value theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/07-financials/omnicapital-financial-group-ltd/master/theme-f-value.md)
