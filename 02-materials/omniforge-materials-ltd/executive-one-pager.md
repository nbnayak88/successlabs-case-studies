# OmniForge Materials — Executive One-Page Brief

> **GICS02 · Fictional educational case · v 1.0 · 2026-10-19.** Source figures are fictional baselines or targets. New names, incidents, options and exercises are illustrative; no achieved outcomes or approved implementation are claimed.

**Decision:** Select a bounded materials-traceability and reliability pilot before committing an enterprise rollout.

**Situation:** USD42 B materials group,46,000 employees,31 countries. Steel/alloys 30%, cement/aggregates 22%, chemicals 20%, mining/minerals 16%, advanced materials 12%.

**Burning platform:** Disconnected mining, production, logistics, quality, sales and recycling;180+ applications; weak material genealogy and manual ESG reconciliation.

**Illustrative hero trigger:** A customer queries a shipped batch's certificate after a laboratory correction, while a kiln reliability alert and uncertain recycled-feedstock records expose the same trust gap.

**Strategic choice:** Compare local pilots, enterprise platform first, and federated value-stream delivery under common data/safety standards. The third is a provisional learning recommendation, subject to evidence.

**Architecture:** Feedstock-to-Qualified-Material; Asset-to-Reliability; Order-to-Trusted-Delivery; Recover-to-Reuse; Evidence-to-Carbon-Claim. Connect capability/process owners to ERP, MES, EAM, LIMS, MDM, edge, APIs and governed AI. Critical control remains within approved plant safety boundaries.

**Investment:** Source categories 230+125+90+55+40 = USD540 M over 36 months. Benefit categories 190+65+55+40 = USD350 M; working-capital release and recurring savings require separation. Predictive-maintenance USD70 M may overlap efficiency. No validated NPV/payback.

**Sequence:** Foundation/governance and asset pilots → integrated data/AI/control tower → digital twins, circularity and controlled optimization. These are source targets, not results.

**Board gates:** Baseline and benefits owner; quality genealogy; safety/cyber evidence; workforce readiness; rollback; downside economics. Decide to scale, redesign or stop using observed pilot evidence.
