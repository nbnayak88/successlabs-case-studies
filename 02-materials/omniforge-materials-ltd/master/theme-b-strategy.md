# Theme B — Define Strategic Direction

> **GICS02 · Fictional educational case · v 1.0 · 2026-10-19.** Enterprise figures are stipulated fictional source baselines or targets. Names, incidents, strategic options and learning exercises are illustrative additions; no actual client implementation or achieved outcome is claimed.

## OMF-M05 — Market, Industry & Competitive Intelligence

Explore customer demand for traceable lower-carbon materials, energy-price exposure, supply disruptions and recycled feedstock availability. These are investigation themes, not independently researched market findings. No market share or competitor ranking is asserted.

**Learning deliverable:** Market hypothesis register and customer evidence plan

**Decision challenge:** What buyer evidence would justify a premium for verified product-carbon information?

**Source locator:** Transformation Drivers; Company Overview. [Evidence register](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).

## OMF-M06 — Strategic Ambition & Transformation Vision

Source vision: resilient, circular, low-carbon materials through intelligent operations, connected value chains and data-driven innovation. Distinguish 35% Scope 1/2 emissions reduction by 2032 from Year 3 carbon-intensity index 100→75; the denominator and boundary differ.

**Learning deliverable:** North Star and outcome/constraint register

**Decision challenge:** Can intensity improve while absolute emissions rise, and what would that mean for the strategy?

**Source locator:** Digital Transformation Vision; Transformation Drivers; KPIs. [Evidence register](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).

## OMF-M07 — Strategic Options & Investment Choices

Illustrative choices: A plant-local pilots; B enterprise platform first; C federated value-stream pilots with common master-data, safety and carbon standards. C is a learning hypothesis requiring evidence, not source-approved investment.

**Learning deliverable:** Weighted options matrix and decision record

**Decision challenge:** What evidence would make you choose B or stop C?

**Source locator:** Aligned blueprint 14.3 and 14.6; illustrative extension. [Evidence register](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).

## OMF-M08 — Transformation Thesis & Value Creation

Trusted asset/material/product data plus safe industrial connectivity and accountable teams should improve reliability, quality, circularity and customer trust. Link each hypothesis to a baseline, owner, control and experiment before recognizing benefit.

**Learning deliverable:** Causal value tree and benefits ownership

**Decision challenge:** Which part of the USD190 M efficiency estimate is genuinely incremental?

**Source locator:** 14.3 Target transformation operating model; Expected Returns. [Evidence register](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).
