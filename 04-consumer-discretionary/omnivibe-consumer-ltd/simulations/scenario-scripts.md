# OmniVibe — 12 Simulation Scripts

> **GICS04 · Fictional educational case · v1.0 · 2026-10-19.** Source figures are fictional enterprise baselines. Names, incidents, strategic options and non-workforce designs are illustrative learning extensions. No implemented client outcome, selected product capability or approved budget is claimed.

## OVC-SIM-01 — CEO discovery
- **Counterparts:** OVC-P01, OVC-P03, OVC-P04.
- **Master:** OVC-M01, OVC-M04, OVC-M06.
- **Objective:** Define a business decision before a platform proposal.
- **Opening:** “What customer promise gets better?”
- **Withheld constraint:** Consumer failure triggers are illustrative; source evidence is workforce-focused; reveal after a relevant question.
- **Follow-up:** Separate known facts from a customer hypothesis.
- **Expected artifact:** Problem statement and evidence request.
- **Debrief:** Needs, evidence, decisions, controls, acceptance and next experiment.
