# OmniForge Materials Ltd. — Enterprise Business Transformation Case Study

> **GICS02 · Fictional educational case · v 1.0 · 2026-10-19.** Source figures are fictional baselines or targets. New names, incidents, options and exercises are illustrative; no achieved outcomes or approved implementation are claimed.

A batch passes through mining, processing, production and distribution, yet the customer cannot verify its quality, recycled content or carbon footprint. Can a materials enterprise create a trusted digital thread while protecting people, plant stability and capital?
