# OmniHealth — 12 Architecture Simulation Scripts

> **GICS06 · Health Care · Fictional educational case · v1.0 · 2026-10-19.** Enterprise figures are source-stipulated baselines or targets. New names, incidents, options and exercises are illustrative. This case is architecture learning, not clinical guidance or evidence of a deployed clinical system.

## OHS-SIM-01 — CEO discovery
- **Counterparts:** OHS-P01, OHS-P03, OHS-P08.
- **Master:** OHS-M01, OHS-M04, OHS-M06.
- **Objective:** Frame coordinated care as a business decision.
- **Opening:** “Which patient journey changes first?”
- **Withheld:** Hero handoff incident is invented; reveal after relevant discovery.
- **Follow-up:** Separate source facts from missing evidence.
- **Artifact:** Problem statement and evidence request.
- **Debrief:** Needs, evidence, authority, privacy/safety boundaries, acceptance and next experiment.
