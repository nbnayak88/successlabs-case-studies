## Evidence labels
- **Fictional case baseline:** stipulated world-building facts from the Notion case, not externally verified.
- **Illustrative assumption:** proposed design choice, economic number, technology option or scenario constraint.
- **Target:** planned future performance, not an achieved result.
- **Hypothesis:** relationship requiring validation.
- **Unvalidated:** missing proof, calculation or source consistency.
