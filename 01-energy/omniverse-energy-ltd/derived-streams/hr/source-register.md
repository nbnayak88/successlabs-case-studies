## Reconciliation policy
This publication is a manual connector-assisted transfer. Existing enterprise narrative synchronization does not automatically cover 13 child-page appendices. reconciliation.json maps each canonical file and exact appended text to its source page and captures read-back status. Original source bodies are protected.

Future reconciliation must read both current revisions and the last verified appendix baseline. Only one side changed: review for consistency then transfer with preflight. Both differ: hold affected appendix and write conflict evidence. Unrelated domains may proceed. No deletion, wholesale page replacement or automatic merging of persona/fact conflicts. Notion lacks atomic compare-and-swap; immediate preflight reduces but cannot eliminate races. Record partial success and retry only after inspecting both destinations. Do not advance any pending baseline.

Unattended execution, recovery and conflict notification for the HR appendices have not been tested. No new schedule is created or claimed. The enterprise job requires an explicit mapping/acceptance update before it can claim HR coverage.
