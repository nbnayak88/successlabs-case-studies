### B — Direction (master 05–08)
**Strategic dilemma:** Should OmniEnergy prioritize executive succession, technical-expert continuity, or emerging renewable and digital leadership? Elena must create one portfolio that addresses immediate continuity risks and long-term capability renewal.

**Discovery questions:** Which worker population and business decision are affected? Which source record proves the problem? Who approves country variants? What must remain safe during a failed handoff? What evidence would make us reject our preferred design?

**Options:** retain and control existing local practice; impose a uniform template; or use a common core with justified local variants. Compare worker impact, control coverage, delivery effort and reversibility. No option is approved merely by appearing here.

### C — Architecture (master 09–12)
**OVE-HR04-CAP01 capability group:** Critical-role continuity; readiness assessment; technical careers. These are abilities; the following sequence is a value stream, not a capability hierarchy.

**OVE-HR04-VS01 value stream:** Identify critical role → assess risk → nominate → validate readiness → develop → rehearse handover. Define a stakeholder-valued exit criterion at each stage before detailed process modeling.

**Information objects:** critical position; readiness horizon; evidence; development action; knowledge-transfer record. Record attribute owner, authoritative source, purpose, access, retention, effective date and quality rule.

**Application responsibility:** Succession owns restricted talent decisions; learning owns validated proficiency; business role owners confirm operational readiness.

**Architecture decision OVE-HR04-ADR01 (proposed):** maintain explicit ownership and governed handoffs rather than uncontrolled copies. HR12 owns transport/recovery patterns; this domain owns its business rules and acceptance. Identity, data, UX and infrastructure views reference the shared [architecture boundaries](operating-standard.md).

### D — Transformation (master 13–16)
**OVE-HR04-REQ01:** every in-scope business change has a unique case/event reference, authorized decision, effective date and traceable downstream outcome. **OVE-HR04-REQ02:** a rejected or delayed change remains visible to an accountable owner; a silent drop cannot count as completion. Define domain-specific fields above before build.

Scale only after documented acceptance. Funding and delivery dates remain TBD; source timelines are learning assumptions.

### V1 — Vision
- **Policy:** Evidence-based readiness, confidential access, fair opportunity, technical-career parity, and no permanent talent labels.

- **Practice:** Calibration, readiness evidence, development follow-through, knowledge-transfer reviews, and access control.
- **Project:** Pilot LNG operations and renewable leadership, then scale by business risk.

Build expert communities, global rotations, university partnerships, external talent networks, and skills-powered leadership ecosystems.
