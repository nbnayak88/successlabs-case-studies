## Transformation thesis
**From equipment manufacturer to connected, service-led industrial ecosystem.** OmniWorks links engineering, manufacturing, installed assets, field service and customer outcomes through a governed digital thread, secure IT/OT convergence, accountable value-stream teams and outcome-based services.
