# Theme E — Execute and Assure

> **GICS02 · Fictional educational case · v 1.0 · 2026-10-19.** Enterprise figures are stipulated fictional source baselines or targets. Names, incidents, strategic options and learning exercises are illustrative additions; no actual client implementation or achieved outcome is claimed.

## OMF-M17 — Enterprise Risk, Security & Compliance

Source priorities: segmented SCADA/PLC networks, allowlisting, MFA jump hosts, role controls, immutable audit trails, partner API security and R&D IP protection. Add scenario controls for contaminated feedstock, mis-certified quality and unsupported carbon claims.

**Learning deliverable:** Risk/control register, OT boundary and incident tabletop

**Decision challenge:** What happens when an apparently trusted laboratory feed contains an invalid result?

**Source locator:** Security & Risk Architecture; AI Governance Framework. [Evidence register](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).

## OMF-M18 — Workforce, Leadership & Change Adoption

46,000 employees plus contractors need shift-compatible training, safety certification and accessible tools. Source links 13 HR cases spanning EC, recruiting/onboarding, performance, succession, rewards, learning, payroll, time, analytics, experience, AI and integration. Child content has not been audited.

**Learning deliverable:** Change impact map, role skills and adoption experiments

**Decision challenge:** How will training work for night-shift operators and contractors with limited device access?

**Source locator:** Employees & Contractors; Change & Capability Building; Workforce Monday. [Evidence register](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).

## OMF-M19 — Delivery Readiness & Transformation Execution

Illustrative pilot: one selected production line and a traceable batch journey; reconcile sensor/lab data, shadow recommendations, validate access, test outage behavior and rollback. No autonomous actuation until plant safety approval.

**Learning deliverable:** Pilot charter, acceptance criteria, test evidence and rollback

**Decision challenge:** What measurable stop condition protects operations when the model drifts?

**Source locator:** Architecture Principles; AI Governance; roadmap. [Evidence register](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).
