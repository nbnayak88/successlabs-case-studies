## Prologue — A delivery milestone is not an outcome
An invented client challenges an on-time release when adoption and service records cannot demonstrate business value. Product, operations and customer-success tools disagree. This prompts discovery, not a claim about an actual client or proven cause.
## Act I — Understand the Enterprise (OTS-M01–04)
Fictional CEO **Anika Rao (OTS-P01)** leads a source-stipulated **USD39B** provider with **98,000employees,41countries and11,500enterprise clients**, headquartered in Bengaluru/San Francisco.
Mix:cloud/platform31%, software25%, cybersecurity18%, AI/data16%, managed services10%. Source problems include fragmented lifecycle tools, bespoke integration, limited safe reuse and late security/observability.
[Enterprise theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/08-information-technology/omnitech-solutions-ltd/master/theme-a-enterprise.md)
## Act II — Choose the Future (OTS-M05–08)
Product chief **Maya Sterling (OTS-P03)** asks what reusable capability solves; customer-success lead **Elena Fischer (OTS-P05)** demands client evidence; CFO **David Chen (OTS-P02)** challenges value.
Compare engagement-local improvements, enterprise platform first and federated product/service pilots with shared standards. Third option provisional.
Source40%faster time-to-value,50%reuse,50%lower critical incident exposure and20%platform revenue growth remain undefined target statements.

## Act III — Architect Secure Reuse (OTS-M09–12)
Source chain: **Discover → Design → Build → Secure → Deploy → Observe → Improve**. Security and observability start at design; the chain must not defer them.
Platform lead **Sofia Tan (OTS-P06)** and architect **Daniel Weber (OTS-P12)** connect Client/Product/Service/Asset/Talent360, product/ITSM/CRM/ERP/DevSecOps/SOC, API/event contracts and telemetry.
**Omar Haddad (OTS-P10)** and **Mei Lin (OTS-P09)** require dependency evidence, provenance, tenant isolation and permitted client context. Source stack patterns are proposals, not verified product choices.
[Architecture theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/08-information-technology/omnitech-solutions-ltd/master/theme-c-architecture.md)
## Act IV — Fund and Sequence Evidence (OTS-M13–16)
Source proposes **USD540M over3years** and **USD290M annual value**. Split, run cost, overlap, margin/cash conversion, phasing and discounting absent; NPV/payback unvalidated.
Year1 standards, data, DevSecOps/observability → Year2 AIOps, customer intelligence, reusable solutions → Year3 AI-native products, operations and marketplace proposals.
Targets:80%priority-product platform adoption;30%faster releases/20%lower incidents;50%recurring platform revenue/40%reuse.
[Transformation theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/08-information-technology/omnitech-solutions-ltd/master/theme-d-transformation.md)
## Act V — Make Change Usable and Recoverable (OTS-M17–19)
SRE lead **Marcus Okoro (OTS-P08)** requires permissions, stop conditions, rollback and restoration evidence. CHRO **Nadia Fernandes (OTS-P07)** asks what people can demonstrate after practice.
Illustrative first pilot is one internal product/service cohort with approved synthetic/non-sensitive data. Test contracts, reviewed code, dependencies, tenant isolation, telemetry and recovery. No client production action or autonomous remediation executed.
[Execution theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/08-information-technology/omnitech-solutions-ltd/master/theme-e-execution.md)
## Act VI — Prove Client Value (OTS-M20–22)
Deployment frequency, time-to-value, SLA, remediation, adoption and recurring revenue require baselines, owners, cohorts and balancing measures.
**50%versus40%reuse** remains unresolved. **20%platform revenue growth** differs from **50%recurring platform revenue share**. Incident count differs from critical exposure; release speed differs from time-to-value. All future figures are targets, not results.
[Value theme](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/08-information-technology/omnitech-solutions-ltd/master/theme-f-value.md)
