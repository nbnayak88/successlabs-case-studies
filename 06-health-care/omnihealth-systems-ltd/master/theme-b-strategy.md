# Theme B — Define Strategic Direction

> **GICS06 · Health Care · Fictional educational case · v1.0 · 2026-10-19.** Enterprise figures are source-stipulated baselines or targets. New names, incidents, options and exercises are illustrative. This case is architecture learning, not clinical guidance or evidence of a deployed clinical system.

## OHS-M05 — Market, Industry & Competitive Intelligence

Investigate access barriers, patient preferences, payer incentives, workforce capacity and outcomes-led partnerships. These are discovery themes drawn from source drivers, not independently researched market claims.

**Learning deliverable:** Access/equity and payer evidence plan.

**Decision challenge:** Whose needs are missing from digitally collected feedback?

**Source locator:** Transformation Drivers; Stakeholder Outcomes. [Evidence](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).

## OHS-M06 — Strategic Ambition & Transformation Vision

Source vision: safer, personalized, equitable care via connected data, intelligent operations and human-centred experience. Ambitions include NPS>65, 25% lower documentation burden and15% operating-margin improvement; definitions and baselines are absent.

**Learning deliverable:** North Star, constraints and outcome register.

**Decision challenge:** How will access and clinician burden improve without compromising safety or equity?

**Source locator:** Digital Transformation Vision; Stakeholder Outcomes. [Evidence](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).

## OHS-M07 — Strategic Options & Investment Choices

Illustrative options: A facility-local pilots; B enterprise platform first; C federated care-journey pilots with common identity, interoperability, safety and privacy controls. C is provisional, not a source-approved decision.

**Learning deliverable:** Options matrix and decision evidence.

**Decision challenge:** What would make a local exception necessary or make the pilot unsafe to scale?

**Source locator:** 14.3 and14.6; illustrative extension. [Evidence](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).

## OHS-M08 — Transformation Thesis & Value Creation

Trusted patient/provider/episode data and coordinated workflows should reduce repetition, improve access and release care capacity. Benefits must be measured with clinical and operational owners; throughput or software adoption alone does not establish improved health outcomes.

**Learning deliverable:** Causal benefits tree and ownership.

**Decision challenge:** Which benefit is incremental and which shifts workload to another team?

**Source locator:** 14.3 Target operating model; Investment & Outcomes. [Evidence](../references/source-and-assumptions.md).
