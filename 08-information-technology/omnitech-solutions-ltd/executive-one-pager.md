# OmniTech — Executive One-Page Brief

> **GICS08 · Information Technology · Fictional educational case · v1.0 · 2026-10-19.** Source figures are fictional enterprise baselines or targets. Names, incidents, options and exercises are illustrative. No approved investment, deployed system, validated product choice or achieved outcome is asserted.

**Decision:** Fund a bounded platform-to-client-outcome pilot with security, recovery and economic evidence before wider rollout.

**Situation:** USD39B technology provider;98,000employees;41countries;11,500clients. Cloud/platform31%, software25%, cyber18%, AI/data16%, managed services10%.

**Source problem:** Fragmented lifecycle tooling, bespoke integrations, poor data/AI reuse and late security/observability create quality and margin pressure.

**Illustrative trigger:** A client disputes an 'on-time' delivery milestone when adoption and service evidence do not demonstrate business value. Teams cannot reconcile release, incident and customer-success records. This is invented discovery context.

**Options:** Local engagement improvements; enterprise platform first; federated product/service pilots on shared engineering, data, security and telemetry standards. Third option provisional.

**Architecture:** Discover→Design→Build→Secure→Deploy→Observe→Improve. Controls start at design despite chain order. Source Client/Product/Service/Asset/Talent360, domain tools, APIs/events, telemetry and cloud/hybrid patterns require validation and ownership.

**Economics:** ProposedUSD540M over3years; USD290Mannual value assumption. Split, costs, overlap, margin/cash conversion and discounting absent; no validated NPV/payback.

|Source target|Evidence gap|
|---|---|
|Year1:80%priority products on shared platform|Priority population and effective adoption|
|Year2:30%faster release;20%lower incidents|Baseline, method, severity and cohort|
|Year3:50%recurring platform revenue;40%reuse|Share denominator and reuse definition|
|Other:40%faster time-to-value;50%reuse;50%lower exposure;20%platform revenue growth|Different measures/scopes; retain rather than conflate|

**Gates:** Client-data/IP isolation, dependency evidence, reviewed outputs, observability, recovery, workforce readiness and verified client value.
