# Hero Story — The Shelf Gap and the Held Batch

> **GICS05 · Consumer Staples · Fictional educational case · v1.0 · 2026-10-19.** Source enterprise figures are fictional baselines or targets. New names, incidents, options and exercises are illustrative. No actual client implementation, approved investment or achieved outcome is asserted.

## Prologue
An invented promotion raises demand for an everyday essential. A retailer reports empty shelves while a distributor feed still lists stock from a batch on quality hold. These are fictional discovery triggers, not evidence of an actual food-safety incident.

## Act I — Understand
CEO Anika Mehra asks how a USD51B staples enterprise can keep essentials available, affordable and trusted across57countries. Sales, supply, quality and technology must establish which handoff failed. (ODG-M01–04)

## Act II — Choose
Growth chief Marcus Reed demands service; quality director David Okoro protects release authority. CFO James Whitfield compares local pilots, platform-first investment and federated demand-to-shelf experiments. The third option remains conditional. (ODG-M05–08)

## Act III — Architect
Supply chief Kavya Rao and architect Priya Nair trace demand, ingredient/product records, batch release, shipment and outlet evidence. Data chief Sofia Alvarez and CISO Omar Haddad require governed identities, consent and trust boundaries. (ODG-M09–12)

## Act IV — Fund evidence
The source proposes490M over36months and400M annual benefit categories. Finance asks how revenue becomes margin, cash and attributable benefit without counting maintenance twice. Foundation, acceleration and transformation need dependency gates. (ODG-M13–16)

## Act V — Make it work
Plant director Elena Petrović requires rollback; field director Rohan Shah tests offline partner updates; CHRO Nandita Sen creates role-based practice. Quality holds must remain authoritative even when a stale feed arrives later. (ODG-M17–19)

## Act VI — Prove value
Forecast82%, shelf availability97%, yield97% andautomation70% are Year3targets, not achievements. The board uses pilot evidence to scale, redesign or stop. No historical success is invented. (ODG-M20–22)
