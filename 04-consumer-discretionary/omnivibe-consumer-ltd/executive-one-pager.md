**Decision requested:** Select one brand/country workforce-to-service pilot and evidence gates before approving enterprise scale.

**Situation:** USD 28B consumer group;72,000 employees;48 countries. Fashion 32%,beauty 18%,home 17%,specialty retail 15%,travel 10%,marketplace 8%. Source network:3,200 stores,14 e-commerce sites,26 DCs and140 micro-fulfillment sites.

**Source problem:** Fragmented workforce data, talent journeys, HR portals and payroll/time integrations undermine readiness and decision quality. The source does not document a historical customer incident.

**Illustrative trigger:** A customer arrives for a promised collection; channel information and store readiness disagree. A new associate also lacks a completed onboarding/skills handoff. These invented events prompt discovery rather than establish causality.

**Strategy options:** Brand-local pilots; central platform first; federated journey experiments with common workforce-data, privacy and integration standards. Third option is provisional.

**Target architecture:** Connect the source 13workforce journeys to business capabilities, data owners and controlled integrations. Source proposes SuccessFactors modules, Work Zone, People Analytics, Joule and BTP/Integration Suite; validate availability, licensing and country fit before design approval.

**Source sequence:** Wave1 core HR/payroll/time/integration → Wave2 talent lifecycle → Wave3 learning/rewards → Wave4 intelligence/experience/AI. Dates and durations are not supplied.

**Economics:** Investment, recurring costs, benefits and quantitative targets remain TBD. Require pilot costs, baseline evidence, owners and downside scenarios. No ROI/NPV/payback claim.
